Privacy policy — Rclone Personal Backup

Effective 27 October 2026.

This personal application connects Rclone to the owner's Google Drive account for encrypted backup storage and recovery. It is not offered to other users.

Google data accessed and used

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can create, list, read, update, and delete only Drive files used with this application. Rclone uses that access to upload encrypted backup objects, verify them, and restore them when the owner requests recovery. The application does not request access to all Drive files.

Storage and sharing

Backup objects are encrypted before upload and stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are kept on an owner-controlled device. The application has no analytics or advertising service. It does not sell data or share it with anyone other than Google as the storage and authentication provider.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future backup and restore access until the owner authorizes it again.
